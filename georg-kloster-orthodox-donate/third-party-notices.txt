## PDF libraries

The distribution under includes/lib/dompdf includes full upstream PHP sources
and Composer metadata. No executable code is downloaded at runtime.
Only the WordPress autoload entry wrapper is adapted with an ABSPATH guard;
upstream files under vendor are not modified.
